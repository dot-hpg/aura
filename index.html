        Questions

        Before you decide.

        Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp and an engineer will reply.

        Can I automate my home if it's already built?

        Yes. Most smart switches fit into your existing switch boxes and wiring, so there's no need to break walls. For new builds we recommend planning automation during construction for the cleanest result.

        Will my switches still work if the internet goes down?

        Yes. The touch panels on the wall keep working as normal switches. You only need internet to control things from your phone when you're away.

        Does it work with Alexa and Google Assistant?

        Yes. We connect your switches, lights and curtains to Alexa or Google so you can control them by voice and create scenes.

        How long does installation take?

        A single room is usually done the same day. Full-home projects depend on the size and the stage of construction, and we share a timeline with your quotation.

        What about warranty and service?

        All products come with manufacturer warranty, and our smart switches carry up to 5 years. After handover, you call one number for support.

        Do you work with architects, builders and hotels?

        Yes. We plan automation with architects and interior designers, and take up bulk projects for builders, offices and hotels.
